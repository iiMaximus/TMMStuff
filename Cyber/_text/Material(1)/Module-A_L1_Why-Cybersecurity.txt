  What do we mean by “system”?
    Security is no longer local, it is systemic, but … what do we mean
    by “system”?
    A system is not just a single device or application. A system is a
    set of interacting components that:
    •    perform a function
    •    exchange information
    •    and depend on each other to deliver a service                Hardware
                                                                                 Software
    In cybersecurity, the system includes:
    •    hardware and software
    •    networks and data                                    Procedures
    •    users and operators
    •    procedures and dependencies                                                 People

A.A. 2025 - 2026
  IT systems and OT systems
    • IT (Information Technology) systems manage data,
      computation, and communication (e.g., servers, databases,
      cloud services)
    • OT (Operational Technology) systems control physical
      processes (e.g., industrial controllers, sensors, actuators)
    Modern infrastructures combine IT and OT, creating new
    dependencies, new attack paths, real-world physical consequences

    ➢ protect networks, information systems, computer services, and

  Cybersecurity -- Definition 3 (national-scale)
    Practice that allows an entity (organization, citizen, nation, ...) to
    protect:
       ➢ its physical assets

       ➢ confidentiality, integrity and availability of its information

    from threats that come from cyberspace.
                                                 [standard ISO/IEC 27000:2014 & ISO/IEC 27032:2012]


    This definition connects cybersecurity to national-scale assets and
    cyber-physical impact.

  National Cyberspace
    ➢ The segment of the global network that falls within the nation’s
      legal and administrative purview.
    ➢ Interlinked with the cyberspace, is distinct in its governance,
      under the jurisdiction of national laws and regulations, and is
      shaped by the country’s unique economic, cultural, and political
      context.

A.A. 2025 - 2026
  National Cyberspace
    • Can be seen as a portion of the cyberspace, defined by the
      digital infrastructures within a nation’s geographical boundaries
      and by its interconnected citizen, businesses and government.
    • It includes all devices operating within the nation; the software
      running on these devices; the data generated, stored, or
      transiting through this infrastructure.
    • National cyberspace is where security becomes a matter of
      sovereignty, law, and coordinated response.
