   The Issue
    ➢ unfortunately, unlike what you might think, in a computing
      system, when you “delete” a file, you don't actually physically
      delete anything, you simply ask the Operating System to delete
      that file…or, more precisely, you inform it that you are not going
      to use that file any longer
    ➢ thereinafter, the Operating System assumes that the space
      previously occupied by the deleted file is available and therefore
      it can “rewrite” it, when needed
    ➢ however, the file is still physically stored on the disk and as long
      as it is not overwritten, it can easily be recovered

   Degaussing
    ➢    Is the removal or reduction of a magnetic field of a hard disk or
         drive, using a device called a degausser that has been
         specifically designed for the media being erased




Cybersecurity and National Defence (Grandi Sfide - Digital)
                                                                             30
A.Y. 2025 - 2026
   Degaussing Side Effects
    ➢ applied to magnetic media, degaussing may purge an entire
      media element quickly and effectively
    ➢ degaussing often renders hard disks inoperable, as it erases
      low-level formatting that is only done at the factory during
      manufacturing
    ➢ in some high-security environments, one may be required to use
      a degausser that has been approved for the task
         ➢ for example, in US government and military jurisdictions,

            one may be required to use a degausser from the NSA’s
            “Evaluated Products List”

   Physical Access Attacks
    ➢ the attacker has physical access to the target system
    ➢ physical access bypasses the strongest digital defenses
      (encryption, firewalls, and 2FA) by attacking the environment
      rather than the network
    ➢ attackers exploit social norms (politeness, authority, and
      urgency) to walk through the front door
    ➢ once inside, an attacker only needs a few seconds of “hands-on”
      time to compromise a system permanently

                    “If a bad actor has unrestricted physical access to
                    your computer, it's not your computer anymore.”
