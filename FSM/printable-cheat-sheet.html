Trusses joints

Ideal truss member
\[ N\neq 0,\qquad T_y = 0,\qquad M_z = 0 \]
  1. Solve reactions.
  2. Pick a joint with max two unknown rods.
  3. Assume rods pull away from joint.
  4. Use \(\sum F_x=0\), \(\sum F_y=0\).
45 degree member
\[ N_x = N\cos45^\circ = \frac{N}{\sqrt{2}},\qquad N_y = N\sin45^\circ = \frac{N}{\sqrt{2}} \]
TrapPositive N = tension. Negative N = compression, so check buckling.

Diagrams N, T, M

  • Use the local axes on each member.
  • At a corner, axial/shear directions rotate.
  • At a hinge, moment is zero for an ideal hinge.

Bending stress

\[ \sigma = \frac{N}{A}+\frac{My}{I} \]
T/L/C section
\[ y_{top}=H-y_G,\qquad y_{bottom}=y_G \] \[ n=\frac{S_y}{\max\left(\left|\sigma_{top}\right|,\left|\sigma_{bottom}\right|\right)} \]

Unsymmetric bending

Principal axes
\[ \sigma_z=\frac{N}{A}+\frac{M_1y_1}{I_1}-\frac{M_2x_1}{I_2} \] \[ \text{neutral axis:}\qquad \sigma_z=0 \]
TrapFor L/C sections, the most stressed point may not be obvious until you check corners in principal axes.

Deflection elastic line

\[ EI\,v''(z)=M(z),\qquad \alpha(z)=v'(z) \]
  1. Write M(z) by region.
  2. Integrate to rotation.
  3. Integrate to displacement.
  4. Apply boundary/continuity conditions.
\[ \text{clamp: }v=0,\ \alpha=0;\qquad \text{simple support: }v=0 \] \[ v_L=v_R,\qquad \alpha_L=\alpha_R \]
Cantilever
\[ \theta_F=\frac{FL^2}{2EI},\qquad v_F=\frac{FL^3}{3EI} \] \[ \theta_q=\frac{qL^3}{6EI},\qquad v_q=\frac{qL^4}{8EI} \] \[ v_C=\frac{q a^3(4L-a)}{24EI}\quad\text{partial UDL from wall over }a \]

Shafts initial vs complete yielding

At a shoulder, use small \(d\)
\[ A=\frac{\pi d^2}{4},\qquad I=\frac{\pi d^4}{64},\qquad J=\frac{\pi d^4}{32} \] \[ \sigma_{nom,b}=\frac{32M}{\pi d^3},\qquad \tau_{nom}=\frac{16T}{\pi d^3},\qquad \sigma_{nom,a}=\frac{4N}{\pi d^2} \]
CheckFormulaMeaning
Initial \(\sigma=K_{t,b}\sigma_b+K_{t,a}\sigma_a\)
\(\tau=K_{t,s}\tau_t\)		 Local notch / first yield
Complete \(\sigma=\sigma_b+\sigma_a\)
\(\tau=\tau_t\)		 Whole section yield, use \(K_t=1\)
Von Mises
\[ \sigma_{VM}=\sqrt{\sigma^2+3\tau^2},\qquad n=\frac{S_y}{\sigma_{VM}} \]

3D gear shaft

\[ T=F_tR,\qquad M_b=\sqrt{M_x^2+M_y^2} \] \[ \sigma_b=\frac{32M_b}{\pi d^3},\qquad \tau_t=\frac{16T}{\pi d^3},\qquad \sigma_a=\frac{4N}{\pi d^2} \]
TrapRead separate \(K_t\) for bending, axial, torsion using \(D/d\) and \(r/d\).

Failure Criteria ductile vs brittle

Ductile, principal stresses
\[ \sigma_{eq}^{T}=\max\left(|\sigma_1-\sigma_2|,\ |\sigma_2-\sigma_3|,\ |\sigma_3-\sigma_1|\right) \] \[ \sigma_{eq}^{VM}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \sqrt{(\sigma_1-\sigma_2)^2+(\sigma_2-\sigma_3)^2+(\sigma_3-\sigma_1)^2} \]
Plane stress
\[ \sigma_{VM}=\sqrt{\sigma_x^2+\sigma_y^2-\sigma_x\sigma_y+3\tau_{xy}^2} \] \[ \sigma_{1,2}=\frac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2}\pm \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_x-\sigma_y}{2}\right)^2+\tau_{xy}^2} \]
Simple normal + shear
\[ \sigma_T=\sqrt{\sigma^2+4\tau^2},\qquad \sigma_{VM}=\sqrt{\sigma^2+3\tau^2} \] \[ \text{pure shear:}\qquad \sigma_T=2\tau,\qquad \sigma_{VM}=\sqrt{3}\tau \]
Brittle, Rankine
\[ \sigma_{eq}=\max(\text{principal tensile stresses}),\qquad n=\frac{S_U}{\sigma_{eq}} \]
Strain gauge, plane stress
\[ G=\frac{E}{2(1+\nu)} \] \[ \sigma_x=\frac{E}{1-\nu^2}(\varepsilon_x+\nu\varepsilon_y),\qquad \sigma_y=\frac{E}{1-\nu^2}(\varepsilon_y+\nu\varepsilon_x),\qquad \tau_{xy}=G\gamma_{xy} \]
Microstrain: \(1000\ \mu\mathrm{m}/\mathrm{m}=1000\times10^{-6}=0.001\).

Shear Stress VQ/Ib

\[ \tau=\frac{VQ}{Ib} \]
  • V: shear force at section.
  • Q: first moment of area above/below the point.
  • b: local width.
Thin wall
\[ q_s=\frac{VS}{I},\qquad \tau=\frac{q_s}{t} \]
Checks
\[ \tau_{max,rect}=\frac{3V}{2A},\qquad \tau_{max,circle}=\frac{4V}{3A} \]
TrapOpen sections can twist if load is not through shear centre.

Torsion twist

\[ \tau=\frac{Tr}{J},\qquad \tau_{max}=\frac{TR}{J},\qquad \varphi=\frac{TL}{GJ},\qquad G=\frac{E}{2(1+\nu)} \]

Thin-walled closed

Bredt
\[ q=\frac{T}{2A_m},\qquad \tau=\frac{q}{t}=\frac{T}{2A_m t} \]

Thin-walled open

\[ J_t\approx\sum_i \frac{b_i t_i^3}{3},\qquad \tau_{i,max}\approx\frac{Tt_i}{J_t} \]
Closed thin-walled sections are much stronger in torsion than open sections.

Final Traps do not lose easy points

  • Use N and mm; stress is MPa.
  • Use small diameter at a shaft shoulder.
  • Use J for torsion, not I.
  • For brittle cast iron, use Rankine unless told otherwise.
  • For trusses, shear and bending are zero.
  • For compression rods, check buckling before relaxing.
  • For partial UDL, \(Q_{tot}=qa\), not \(qL\).
  • For open sections, centroid is not necessarily shear centre.
