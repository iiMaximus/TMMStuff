Fatigue
• Failure of materials due to cyclic loading.
• Main reason of mechanical failure of materials
• Failure happens at stress lower than R or Y
• Catastrophic failure of materials (also for 
ductile materials !)
• Fatigue tests: materials are cyclically loaded at
different stresses up to failure.
• Fatigue limit: when cyclically loaded below this
limit, materials do not fail (time considered by 
the graph)
