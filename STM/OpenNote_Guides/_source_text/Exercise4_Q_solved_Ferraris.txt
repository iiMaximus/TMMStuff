Conventionally compressive stress are considered negative while tensile ones as
positive. For this reason frequently, for thermally induced stresses, DT is considered as
T0-T1 in order to obtain negative stress in case of elongation (compression of the
component heated with constrained ends) and positive in case of contraction (tensile
stress on the component cooled with constrained ends). If we consider DT as T1-T0, as
before, in the stress calculation we should remember that in heating we have
elongation (positive) which results, in case of constrained ends, in a compressive stress
on the component, which is negative. In cooling we have a contraction of the material
(negative) which results, in case of constrained ends, in a tensile stress on the
component (positive).
