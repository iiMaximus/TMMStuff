 Proeutectoid alpha(ferrite)
727°C Eutectoid reaction: Austenite -> Ferrite + Cementite 
Pearlite
Main Phases
Ferrite (α) → Solid solution of carbon in α-iron (body-centered 
cubic, BCC).
•Carbon content (maximum 0.022 wt% C).
Austenite (γ) → Solid solution of carbon in γ-iron (face-
centered cubic, FCC).
•Can contain up to 2.14 wt% C.
•Stable between approximately 727°C and 1493°C.
Cementite (Fe₃C) → Hard and brittle intermetallic compound.
•Contains 6.67 wt% C
