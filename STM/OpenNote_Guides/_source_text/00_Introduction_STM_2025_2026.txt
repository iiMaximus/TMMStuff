Information about the STM exam 
(see also the course webpage for more details) 
• The exam is a written one (duration: around 45 min -1 h our) for 
STM, based on what done during lectures and exercise classes 
• The exam will comprise open questions needing a concise  but 
exhaustive response, multiple choice questions, and exercises.
The total score will be 32 (30 cum laude) 
• The final grade for this integrated STM-TMM course  is ONE 
(average of the two STM and TMM grades) 
• STM grade is valid indefinitely until you pass TMM . 
• Warning #1: once you have passed the STM exam (score ≥18), 
it will be considered automatically accepted unless you reject it by 
e-mail by 48 h from publication. 
• Warning #2: you must always book for the STM exam even if 
you already passed STM and have only to give TMM!
