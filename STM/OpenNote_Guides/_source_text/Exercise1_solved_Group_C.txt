and strain you use to calculate).
200
0.7%
At the unloading  after the loading at 200MPa 
we have → recovery of elastic deformation, and 
a permanent deformation: 
p=0.7%=0.007
l=p*l0= 0.007*50=0.35mm 
During loading, the total deformation 𝜀 includes 
both the elastic and plastic parts.
When the load is removed (unloading), the 
elastic deformation recovered, while the plastic 
deformation remains this is the permanent 
deformation of the material.
