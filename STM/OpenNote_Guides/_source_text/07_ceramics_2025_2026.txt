Most ceramics are compounds of non-metallic and metallic el ements for 
which the interatomic bonds can be totally ionic, totally covalent, or mixed.
- Energetically very strong directional bonds 
- No free electrons 
Ceramic (“keramos”) = “burnt stuff”, indicating the method to produce 
these desired proprieties
